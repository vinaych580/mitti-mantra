Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our verified agricultural consulting service.

Are these experts independently verified?

+
Honestly — not yet. This directory is an illustrative catalog for exploring the kinds of local specialists available, not a credential-checked network; we don't want to claim a degree/certification verification process we don't actually run. What is real: every "Verified Outcome" badge you see is built from actual farmers who booked through this app and self-reported what happened — worked, partially helped, or didn't. For an independently verified expert, India's real Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) network has a government agricultural scientist in nearly every district — a genuine, government-run alternative alongside this directory. (KVK links occasionally move — search "Krishi Vigyan Kendra [your district]" if this one has moved.)

How long does it take for an expert to respond or visit?

+
For online consultations, experts usually reply or pick up calls within 15 to 30 minutes. For physical farm visits, consultations are booked for the next day, and arrival timings are calculated based on travel distance from the expert's local service district.

What if no expert is available near my location?

+
If there is no physical expert serving your immediate district, our algorithm automatically recommends online experts who can assist you via phone call, video chat, or photos. You will still receive high-quality support remotely.

Can I reschedule or cancel my consultation request?

+
Yes, you can reschedule or cancel your booking request without any charges up to 2 hours before the scheduled expected arrival time. You can do this from your customer dashboard or by using the support chat.
