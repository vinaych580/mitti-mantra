What’s actually under the hood

Built on data, not guesswork

The yield model scores R² 0.83 on held-out data and the crop recommender 84% top-1 accuracy across 23 crops. Both are trained on this project’s own crop, soil and weather tables and have not been tested on real fields yet — useful for ranking options, not a substitute for your own judgement. The live figures are at /api/ml/status, and every recommendation shows the maths behind it.
