# Data Dictionary

`weather.csv`, `yield_history.csv`, `mandi_prices.csv` and `msp_prices.csv` are
generated by the Phase 1 pipeline (`python -m pipelines.run build`) from public
sources; do not edit them by hand. They cover the 30 pilot regions so a fresh
clone runs with no downloads. Every district the sources share is in
`serving.sqlite` (see the end of this file). Each generated row carries
`source` (where it came from) and `is_synthetic` (always `false`; the build
fails on a synthetic row).

## `soil.csv`

- `region_id`: Region identifier used across datasets.
- `soil_type`: Dominant soil class.
- `ph`: Soil pH (unitless, 0-14).
- `n_kg_per_acre`: Available nitrogen in kg/acre.
- `p_kg_per_acre`: Available phosphorus in kg/acre.
- `k_kg_per_acre`: Available potassium in kg/acre.
- `organic_carbon_percent`: Soil organic carbon percentage.

## `weather.csv`

- `region_id`: Region identifier.
- `season`: Crop season (`kharif`, `rabi`, `annual`).
- `year`: Observation year.
- `rainfall_mm`: Seasonal rainfall in mm.
- `avg_temp_c`: Average temperature in Celsius.
- `min_temp_c`: Minimum observed temperature in Celsius.
- `max_temp_c`: Maximum observed temperature in Celsius.
- `source`, `is_synthetic`: see the top of this file. Source: NASA POWER daily
  (MERRA-2) at the district point, summed per season: kharif Y = Jun-Oct Y,
  rabi Y = Nov Y - Mar Y+1, zaid Y = Mar-May Y. A season with under 90% of its
  days present is left out.

## `yield_history.csv`

- `region_id`: Region identifier.
- `crop_id`: Crop identifier.
- `year`: Production year.
- `season`: Crop season.
- `yield_kg_per_acre`: Yield in kg/acre, in marketable form (see note below).
- `area_acres`: Cultivated area in acres.
- `production_kg`: Total production in kg.
- `source`, `is_synthetic`: see the top of this file. Source: DES Area,
  Production and Yield (district level), last ten crop years. `year` is the
  start of the agricultural year (2022 = kharif 2022 and rabi 2022-23).

Yields everywhere in this dataset are in the form the crop is sold at mandi, so `yield x price_inr_per_kg` is revenue. Turmeric is priced cured (dried, polished), so its yields are cured weight -- about 20% of the fresh rhizome harvested. Every figure is per acre (tobacco's yield was once a per-hectare figure), and prices are what the farmer's produce fetches (cashew is the raw nut, not the kernel; mango is farm-gate, not retail). Coconut is kg of dehusked nuts, about 2 nuts per kg.

## `mandi_prices.csv`

- `region_id`: Region identifier.
- `crop_id`: Crop identifier.
- `date`: Price observation date (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
- `price_inr_per_kg`: Price in INR per kg.
- `market_name`: "<district> district mandis (<n>)": the monthly figure is the
  mean of weekly arrival-weighted modal prices across the district's mandis.
- `source`, `is_synthetic`: see the top of this file. Source: Agmarknet APMC
  arrivals and prices (India Data Portal mirror), converted from Rs/quintal.

## `msp_prices.csv`

- `crop_id`, `crop_name`: crop key and the app's display name.
- `year`: start of the marketing year (2025 = kharif 2025 and rabi 2025-26).
- `season`: `kharif`, `rabi` or `annual`.
- `msp_inr_per_quintal`, `msp_inr_per_kg`: the announced MSP.
- `msp_variety`: the variety the MSP is for (e.g. "Paddy - Common",
  "Cotton - Medium Staple"); sugarcane rows are `FRP`.
- `source`, `is_synthetic`: CACP series 1998 onward; sugarcane FRP from
  `pipelines/reference/sugarcane_frp.csv`. Basmati has no MSP, so it has no row.

## `crop_parameters.csv`

- `crop_id`: Canonical crop key.
- `crop_name`: Display name.
- `season`: Typical crop season.
- `base_yield_kg_per_acre`: Baseline yield for simulation, in marketable form (turmeric: cured).
- `water_requirement_mm`: Seasonal crop water need in mm.
- `npk_n_kg_per_acre`: Recommended nitrogen application.
- `npk_p_kg_per_acre`: Recommended phosphorus application.
- `npk_k_kg_per_acre`: Recommended potassium application.
- `temp_min_c`: Lower bound of suitable temperature.
- `temp_max_c`: Upper bound of suitable temperature.
- `growing_degree_days`: Approximate growing-degree-day requirement.
- `avg_input_cost_inr_per_acre`: Baseline input cost per acre at the yield level of `base_yield_kg_per_acre`: seed or planting material, fertiliser, protection, irrigation and hired plus family labour -- the basis of published farm-economics studies, not a paid-out-inputs-only estimate. Horticultural crops (ginger, chilli, tomato, banana, onion, the melons, cucumber) were corrected to this basis in 2026-09; tests/test_ranking_profitability.py guards it.
- `mandi_price_min_inr_per_kg`: Conservative mandi price band floor.
- `mandi_price_max_inr_per_kg`: Conservative mandi price band ceiling.

## `serving.sqlite`

Read-only store built by the pipeline and read through `utils/store.py`.
It is not in git: each build is published as an asset of the `data-store`
GitHub release, named in `pipelines/manifest.json` under `serving`, and
`node scripts/fetch_serving_store.cjs` (the Vercel build command) fetches it,
given a `DATA_STORE_TOKEN` with read access to the repository
(served at `/api/v1/districts/...`). One row per district in `districts`
(LGD id `"<state>-<district>"`, the legacy `region_id` for the 30 pilots, a
map point and which datasets cover it). Data tables: `yield_dcy` (last ten
years), `price_district_weekly` (last 104 weeks), `weather_season`,
`weather_stage` (last three seasons), `monsoon_onset`, `soil_district`
(Soil Health Card shares of tested samples per rating class),
`irrigation_share`, `cost_state_crop` (state level), `msp`, plus `meta`
(rows and data-through date per table).

To keep the store small, rows carry a `source_id` into the `provenance`
table instead of repeating source, licence and fetch time, and prices are
stored as integer paise (`*_paise_kg`); `utils/store.py` returns rupees
(`*_inr_kg`) and the API attaches the provenance rows under `sources`.
